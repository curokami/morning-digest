# ADR: Use Medium RSS as the initial Source

Status: Accepted

## Context
The immediate use case is new Medium articles; multi-source support would expand MVP scope.

## Decision
Version 1 SHALL support Medium RSS only. Domain concepts SHALL remain Medium-independent.

## Consequences
Small MVP and simple discovery; content enrichment may still be required.

Accepted ADRs are normally preserved. A later ADR may supersede this decision.
